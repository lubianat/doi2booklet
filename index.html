📚 DOI to Booklet

Convert open access papers into printable booklets

How it works

  1. Enter a DOI or URL of an open access paper
  2. The tool fetches the PDF from the publisher
  3. Pages are rearranged into booklet format (2-up layout)
  4. Download your printable booklet PDF

Example DOI to try:

10.1371/journal.pone.0154556

Note: This tool works with open access papers. Due to CORS restrictions, some publishers may not allow direct PDF access.
