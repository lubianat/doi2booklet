📚 DOI to Booklet

Convert open access papers into printable booklets

How it works

  1. Enter a direct PDF URL of an open access paper
  2. The tool fetches the PDF from the URL
  3. Pages are rearranged into booklet format (2-up layout)
  4. Download your printable booklet PDF

Example PDF URL to try:

https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4851331/pdf/pone.0154556.pdf

Note: This tool requires a direct link to a PDF file. DOI-to-PDF resolution is not supported. Due to CORS restrictions, some publishers may not allow direct PDF access, and requests may be routed through a third-party proxy.
